Scorekeeper Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 10, 2026

Scorekeeper is a score tracker for board games, card games, sports, and other activities. This policy explains what data the app touches and where it goes.

Your game data stays on your device

Player names, games, round history, and button presets are stored only in local storage on your device. There is no account to create, no sign-in, and no server that this data is ever sent to. If you delete the app, or use "Clear All Data" in Settings, that data is gone — we have no copy of it anywhere, because we never had one.

Advertising (native iOS/Android app only)

The native iOS and Android app (not the web/PWA version) shows a small banner ad, served by Google AdMob. To do this, AdMob's SDK can collect device and advertising identifiers and usage data for the purpose of serving and measuring ads, and — on iOS — will ask for App Tracking Transparency permission before doing anything that requires it. This processing is governed by Google's Privacy Policy, and the exact data types collected are published in Google's own AdMob documentation. You can purchase "Remove Ads" (see below) to turn off ads entirely.

In-app purchase: "Remove Ads"

The optional "Remove Ads" purchase is processed by Apple's App Store or Google Play, and the purchase/entitlement is managed on our behalf by RevenueCat. RevenueCat receives the transaction data needed to verify your purchase and keep ads turned off across app launches; it does not receive any of your game data (players, scores, round history), since that never leaves your device. See RevenueCat's Privacy Policy for details on their handling of purchase data.

What we don't do

  • No account creation, no login, no user profiles
  • No analytics or crash reporting SDKs
  • No selling or sharing of your game data — it never reaches us in the first place

Children's privacy

Scorekeeper is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect personal information from them.

Changes to this policy
